# Project Governance

KRE SOLUTION ENGINEERING LLC owns the project and retains authority over intellectual property, licensing, publication, branding, budgets and external commitments.

Material changes require tests, recorded provenance and review by someone other than the author. Reviewers may pass, conditionally pass or fail a change; release authority remains with the project owner. Security findings cannot be waived by the author of the change. Contributor and maintainer authority may expand through documented project-owner decisions as the community develops.
